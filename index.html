How it works

  1. It catches torrent links

    Click a magnet or .torrent link on any site and choose Open in TorrentGlide, or let the browser handle it as usual.

  2. Your computer downloads it

    The torrent service, the free and open-source rqbit, joins the swarm on your computer and fetches the pieces under the playhead first.

  3. You watch right away

    The player decodes MKV, HEVC, AVI and more in the browser, with subtitles and season packs. Seek ahead without waiting for the rest.
